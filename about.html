THE PROCESS

I'm Katelyn, a student artist exploring the space between everyday life and the images we use to understand it.

My practice is still growing, and I like that it can move between different forms. Photography helps me slow down and pay attention to light, atmosphere, and details that might otherwise pass unnoticed. Digital media gives me another way to experiment—with distortion, remixing, visual references, and the ways meaning changes when an image is moved into a new context.

I'm drawn to work that feels thoughtful but still leaves room for play. I enjoy combining careful observation with unexpected choices, and I see each project as a chance to ask a question rather than arrive at a perfect answer. Sometimes the process is planned; other times, an accident or a small visual detail becomes the starting point.

This portfolio gathers projects from my ART 74 coursework alongside other creative experiments. Together, they document what I'm learning about making images, communicating ideas, and developing a visual language of my own. I hope the work invites viewers to look a little longer, make their own connections, and find something familiar in an unexpected place.
